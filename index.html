18 NOVEMBER · A LITTLE LETTER, JUST FOR YOU
B

My dear Bidisha,

Happy National Princess Day, my lovely princess. ♡

If I could send you a little kingdom in an envelope, this is what I would put inside: mornings that begin gently, laughter that stays a little longer, and enough wonder to make even an ordinary day feel like a story worth keeping.

I chose these photographs because I wanted this world to feel like you — your smile, your beautiful moments, and the places that became part of your story. Every book here is a different way of wishing something lovely for you.

May you have adventures that make your eyes light up. May your dreams find open doors. May you always have people beside you who listen carefully, celebrate you freely, and remind you how much you matter.

And on the days that feel less like a fairytale, I hope you remember this: you do not have to shine every minute to deserve tenderness. You are allowed to rest, to begin again, and to take your own sweet time.

There are so many pages still waiting for you, Bidisha. I hope they are filled with your favourite colours, unexpected happiness, brave little beginnings, and the kind of love that makes you feel at home.

So here is my wish for you today: may you never forget that you are worth the care, the effort, the beautiful words — and an entire little website made just to bring a smile to your face.

Here’s to your own kind of magic.
Today, tomorrow, and every chapter after.
With so much love, always. ♡

ONE OF ONE · FOR BIDISHA
