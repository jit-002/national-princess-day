## 🌟 Live Site
- **GitHub Pages:** [https://jit-002.github.io/national-princess-day/](https://jit-002.github.io/national-princess-day/)

---

## 📖 Features & Interactivity
- **The Voyage & Her Kingdom:** Interactive story sequence with sound and fluid animations.
- **Enchanted 3D Library:** Twelve personal books with three passages each.
  - Click a book spine to select it, then click its cover or *Open this little world*.
  - Browse with arrows, keyboard arrows (`←` / `→`), or horizontal drag gestures.
- **The Sealed Letter:** Scroll vertically past the library to find the wax-sealed paper envelope. Click the seal to unfold the letter, or fold it back to replay.
- **Make a Wish:** Wishes are saved privately in your browser's local storage.
- **Accessibility:** Sound toggle and pause/reduced motion options included.

## 💻 Run Locally
1. Ensure [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) is installed.
2. Start the local server:
   ```bash
   node server.cjs
   ```
3. Open [http://127.0.0.1:4180](http://127.0.0.1:4180) in your browser.
